# MavenLex Legal Pages Drafts

Before real users, add these pages to the website and review them with a lawyer.

## Required pages

- User Agreement
- Privacy Policy
- AI Legal Disclaimer
- Document Processing Policy
- Refund Policy if payments are enabled

## AI disclaimer draft

MavenLex is an AI-assisted legal information tool. It helps users understand documents, risks, legal situations and possible next steps. MavenLex does not replace a licensed lawyer and does not provide final legal advice. Important decisions should be verified with a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Document processing draft

Users should not upload highly sensitive documents unless they understand how the service processes files. Production versions should include document retention controls, delete-my-data controls and clear processing notices.
