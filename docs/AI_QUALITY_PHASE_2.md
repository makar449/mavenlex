# AI Quality Phase 2 — Human Legal Assistant Behavior

This release focuses on AI answer quality, not new product features.

## What changed

- Stronger legal-assistant prompt for live YandexGPT reasoning.
- AI is instructed to answer the exact user question, not a generic template.
- AI distinguishes before-signing vs already-signed situations.
- AI gives priorities: what to fix first, second, third.
- AI includes confidence reasoning when possible.
- AI asks clarifying questions when important facts are missing.
- AI chat supports Smart / Quick / Deep / Action modes.
- Local fallback chat is more human and practical if live AI is unavailable.
- Report now supports AI priorities, decision paths, confidence notes and clarifying questions.
- Longer, staged thinking state makes it clear that the assistant is reading the report and preparing a practical answer.

## Safe legal positioning

The assistant provides informational AI analysis and helps prepare questions, negotiation points and next steps. It does not replace a licensed lawyer and does not provide final legal advice.
