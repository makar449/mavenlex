# MavenLex v6.0.2 — ChatGPT-like Legal Reasoning

This release strengthens MavenLex so AI answers feel like a live, intelligent legal conversation instead of a rigid template.

## Improvements
- Adds adaptive answer depth: short follow-up, draft request, article question, contract question, or deep legal situation.
- Adds stronger ChatGPT-like legal reasoning instructions across contract chat, article chat, and general legal questions.
- Adds quality regeneration when the AI answer sounds like a refusal, generic disclaimer, or weak canned reply.
- Improves local fallback language for messy user questions.
- Adds `/api/ai/chatgpt-like-legal-reasoning-check` and `npm run chatgpt-like-legal-check`.

## Product goal
MavenLex should feel like a strong human-style AI legal conversation inside the website: contextual, practical, calm, direct and useful.
